# You Can Write a Book

<div class="split" markdown="1">
<div markdown="1">

<p class="lede">A live, interactive show about starting. In fifty minutes, you'll start writing.</p>

Everyone gets a notebook and a pen. The audience picks cards, and the cards decide what I talk about, and in what order. By the end you'll have started something, and you'll know how to carry on.

## Where it has been

- **Edinburgh Festival Fringe**, 17–29 August 2026. Greenside @ Riddles Court, Clover Studio. [Listing on edfringe.com](https://www.edfringe.com/tickets/whats-on/you-can-write-a-book).
- **London work-in-progress shows**, Cavendish Arms, Stockwell, June 2026.
